Course Description:
This course explores advanced topics in artificial intelligence, including machine learning algorithms, neural networks, natural language processing, computer vision, and ethical considerations in AI. Students will engage in hands-on labs and a final project to apply theoretical concepts to real-world problems.

Prerequisites:
- AI-101: Introduction to Artificial Intelligence
- CS-201: Programming in Python
- MATH-301: Linear Algebra and Probability

Grading Policy:
- Final Project: 50% (Group or individual project demonstrating AI application)
- Labs: 30% (Weekly assignments on implementing AI techniques)
- Midterm Exam: 20% (In-class exam covering foundational concepts)

Attendance Policy:
Attendance is mandatory for all lectures and labs. More than 3 unexcused absences may result in a grade penalty.

Required Textbooks:
- Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach (4th Edition) by Stuart Russell and Peter Norvig
- Deep Learning by Ian Goodfellow, Yoshua Bengio, and Aaron Courville

Additional Resources:
- Online: Coursera - Machine Learning by Andrew Ng
- Software: Python 3.x, TensorFlow, PyTorch, scikit-learn

Schedule Outline:
- Week 1-2: Introduction to AI and Search Algorithms
- Week 3-5: Machine Learning Basics (Supervised, Unsupervised)
- Week 6: Midterm Exam
- Week 7-9: Neural Networks and Deep Learning
- Week 10-12: NLP and Computer Vision
- Week 13-14: Ethics in AI and Advanced Topics
- Week 15: Final Project Presentations

Academic Integrity:
All work must be original. Plagiarism or cheating will result in a failing grade and possible disciplinary action.
